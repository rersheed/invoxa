Built for the way you already work

Works offline

Create the invoice, the PDF, and the message with no connection. WhatsApp only needs a network when you choose to send.

Branded PDFs

Four templates. Your logo, colours, and bank details, or a service’s own brand. Old invoices keep the snapshot from the day you issued them.

WhatsApp sharing

INVOXA writes a message you can edit, then opens WhatsApp with the text. The PDF is shared on its own. Nothing is sent until you tap send.

Payment tracking

Record each payment yourself: transfer, cash, POS, card, or other. Partial payments stay visible. Statuses are yours — they are not checked with a bank.

Reminders

A local reminder before the due date, on the day, or after it. Paying the invoice cancels the ones still waiting.

Reports

Invoiced, received, outstanding, and overdue stay separate. Filter by today, week, month, year, or a range you choose. Export PDF or CSV on the phone.

Backup you can hold

Save a versioned file, with logos, to a folder or share it. Optional password encryption. Android’s own backup is turned off on purpose.

Install on your phone

  1. Tap Download APK and wait for INVOXA.apk to finish.
  2. Open the file from your downloads.
  3. If Android asks, allow the browser or files app to install unknown apps. You can turn that off again afterwards.
  4. Confirm the install, then open INVOXA.
  5. Add your business, your colours, and the account customers should pay. Then create the first invoice.

The download is the latest release asset: github.com/rersheed/invoxa/releases/latest/download/INVOXA.apk. This page is the download page only. The app itself does not run in the browser.

Your data stays on the device

INVOXA has no account, no server, and no analytics. Invoices, customers, logos, and payments are stored on the phone. A backup is a file you choose to keep. If the phone is lost or reset and you do not have that file, the records are gone. Payment statuses are what you record. They are not a confirmation from your bank.
